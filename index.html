Why work directly

No middleman between your content and the translator working on it

Most translation agencies you might hire are using a freelance translator anyway — they're just taking a cut along the way. Working directly changes three things.

1

(Much) better quality

A dedicated translator gets to know your business and becomes part of your team, not a name pulled from a database. Style and terminology stay consistent from one project to the next.

2

Better value for money

Cutting the intermediary means faster turnaround and a more direct rate, without touching quality — the work is exactly the same, there's just one fewer party billing for it.

3

Direct contact, always

One point of contact, one relationship you can actually rely on. No brief lost in translation between a project manager and the person doing the work.

Services

Beyond translation

Core linguistic work, plus the services that have grown around it as more of what companies produce is machine- or AI-generated first and needs a trained eye afterwards.

Core linguistic services

Translation & Localisation

English into French (France), written for a French reader rather than transposed word for word.

Editing & Proofreading

Revision of human-translated content, scored against your own criteria and delivered at a single quality standard.

LQA — Formal Quality Audit

Formal review of another linguist's work, or of machine and AI output, scored against your LQA model and reported by error category.

AI Post-Editing & Optimization

MTPE of machine- or AI-generated French, including checks for fluent-sounding hallucinated content, transcreation of AI marketing copy, and benchmarking across AI engines.

Additional services

Localization & Functionality Testing

Checking that translated strings still fit the interface they live in, and that the UI/UX behaves as intended once translated.

Prompt Engineering

Designing and calibrating the system prompt that keeps AI output aligned with your style guide, terminology and tone, batch after batch.

Language Data Management

Structuring translation memories and glossaries for reliable AI use — cleaning, RAG curation, and correcting AI output so the model improves.

Multimedia AI

Checking and correcting AI-generated subtitles and voice output — ASR subtitle correction, and dubbing/voice-over review for pronunciation, timing and register.

Post-DTP Proofreading

A final check on the PDF or formatted file once it's typeset — wording, terminology and layout read the way they should before it reaches your client.

Subtitling (as needed)

  • Transcription + time-coding, when a script is provided
  • Translation only, without time-coding
  • Transcription + full time-coding, when no script is provided
